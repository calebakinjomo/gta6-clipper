A vertical GTA 6 Short cut by an AI agent from IShowSpeed's launch stream: Speed starts his first mission, the boat launches onto Vice City's turquoise water, and it looks just like Florida. The clip carries make.fun's pitch — make clips like this with AI and earn money — and is entered in make.fun's $500 "Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views" bounty, where places go by YouTube views. The video goes up on the GTA 6 Secrets channel as a public Short; `VIDEO.json` names its address and `clip.mp4` is the rendered clip. `main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output` for the gate.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
